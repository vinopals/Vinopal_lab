I have been always interested in a broad question of how cells and organisms function when they are subject to seemingly strict rules of chemistry and physics, but at the same time can perform all the miracles of life. While hating chemistry until the third year of the high school, I was suddenly totally consumed by the beauty of biochemistry, explaining to me for the first time, how life blossoms from all the complex chemical reactions and particle/wave interactions, which however all start from rather simple chemical molecules. Biochemistry was then my first choice to study. In the second year of my studies at the now Univesity of Chemistry in Prague, I was introduced to genetic engineering which has become my main trade, running as an Ariadne thread through all of my research.
